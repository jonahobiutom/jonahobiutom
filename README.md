# Jonah Obiutom

Founder of [Tervaq](https://tervaq.com).

I build systems around a simple idea: AI becomes much more useful when it can move beyond answering questions and actually work across the tools, computers, and infrastructure people already use — without taking control away from them.

Most of my work sits somewhere between product, systems engineering, infrastructure, security, and interface design. I like staying close to the whole thing.

## What I’m working on

**Kivren**  
A collaborative computing environment for people and AI agents. I’m interested in making agents useful beyond chat: able to work inside real projects, across browsers and computers, while remaining observable and controllable.

**Obscure OS**  
An exploration of what an AI-native operating environment should feel like when the assistant, applications, runtimes, and workspaces are designed together rather than added as separate layers.

**Tervaq Cloud**  
Infrastructure for deploying and operating software with less friction, with a strong focus on reliability, recovery, observability, and clean operational tooling.

**SalesIQ**  
A business workspace built around the idea that AI should remove repetitive operational work without turning the product into another heavy enterprise dashboard.

I also spend a lot of time on security and fraud research, computer-use infrastructure, browser automation, agent orchestration, and the less glamorous reliability work that determines whether an AI system is genuinely useful outside a demo.

## How I work

I tend to work end-to-end.

That might mean shaping a product direction, tracing a runtime problem, designing an interaction, reviewing infrastructure, or testing the actual system on a machine. I care about architecture, but I care just as much about whether the finished thing feels simple and dependable to the person using it.

The recurring questions for me are usually:

- Can the system do real work rather than just describe it?
- Can a person understand what the system is doing?
- Can they stay in control when it matters?
- Does it remain reliable once the demo is over?
